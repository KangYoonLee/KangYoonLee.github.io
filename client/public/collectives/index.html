What this model does—and doesn't do

This tool illustrates abstract, deterministic communication schedules and physical routing. It does not execute NCCL, infer NCCL channels/protocols, model PCIe/NVLink, simulate NIC queuing or PFC, or predict real-time congestion. For research, compare with instrumented NCCL traces and switch/NIC counters.
